B. Moore site — two-part download
================================
Unzip BOTH into the SAME parent folder so they merge:

  unzip bmoore-site-part1.zip
  unzip bmoore-site-part2.zip

You get one folder: bmoore-site/
Open: bmoore-site/index.html

Part1 = pages, styles, fonts, most images, fashion assets, skyline video.
Part2 = director reel (motion-reel.mp4), still-portrait.mp4, and a few large gallery/frame images that did not fit in part1.
Same relative paths; unzip merges cleanly. No duplicate filenames across the two zips.
